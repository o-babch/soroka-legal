# Soroka Terms of Use

_Effective 2026-08-22. Published at

Soroka is an expense tracker for households, published by Oleksii Babchenko
(sole developer, Spain). Using the app means agreeing to these terms. The short
version: pay through your app store if you subscribe, one subscription covers
your whole household, cancel any time through the store, and check what the AI
read before you save it.

## The service

Soroka records shared and personal expenses for a household. It can read
receipts with an AI model. The AI does its best and is sometimes wrong - the
app always shows you what it read **before** anything is saved, and manual
entry always works. You are responsible for checking that saved amounts match
your receipts; Soroka is a household notebook, not accounting, tax or financial
advice.

## Accounts and households

You sign in with Google or Apple. A household is shared: members you invite see
the household's shared expenses, shopping lists and reports. Only invite people
you want to share with. You can leave a household, and you can delete your
account and its data from inside the app at any time (see the
[account deletion page](../delete-account/)).

## Subscriptions

Soroka's paid plan is bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play, at the
price the store shows when you buy. Payment, renewal, refunds and receipts are
handled by the store under its own terms.

- **One subscription covers the whole household.** Any one member subscribing
  entitles every member; nobody else in the household needs to pay.
- **Every new household starts with a free trial period** (currently 60 days)
  with everything included and no payment details needed. The trial starts by
  itself; it does not convert into a paid subscription unless you subscribe.
- **After the trial**, the free plan remains: manual entry, reports and
  insights stay available, and AI receipt reading is limited each month
  (the current limit is shown in the app).
- **Auto-renewal**: subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your
  store account's subscription settings (App Store: Settings -> Apple ID ->
  Subscriptions; Google Play: Menu -> Payments and subscriptions).
  Cancellation stops the next renewal; access continues to the end of the paid
  period.
- **Refunds** are granted by Apple or Google under their policies; Soroka
  cannot issue or deny store refunds.
- Prices can change for future renewal periods; the store notifies you and asks
  for consent where its rules require it.
