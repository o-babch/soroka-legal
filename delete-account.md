# Delete your Soroka account

This page is for **Soroka: Shared Expenses**, published by Oleksii Babchenko.

## In the app

1. Open Soroka and sign in.
2. Tap the gear icon in the header of any tab to open **Settings**.
3. Scroll to **Delete account**.
4. Type the confirmation the screen asks for, and confirm.

Deletion happens straight away. There is nothing to wait for and no request to
approve.

## By email, if you cannot open the app

Write to **<alekseykpi@gmail.com>** from the address you signed in with, and ask
for your account to be deleted. It is handled the same way and confirmed back to
you.

## What is deleted

Permanently and immediately: your account, your Google or Apple sign-in identity,
your email address, your display name, and every session token belonging to you.

## What is kept, and why

Expenses, item lines, shopping-list entries and receipt images that are already in
your household **stay with the household**, detached from your identity and shown
as belonging to a former member.

This is deliberate. A household's records are shared, and erasing everything one
person entered would rewrite the other person's own financial history - months of
their totals would change because somebody else left. Detaching the identity
satisfies erasure without doing that.

**If you are the last member of the household**, there is nobody left for the
records to belong to, so the household is deleted outright - every expense, every
shopping-list entry, and every receipt image with it.

## Retention after deletion

The live database holds nothing of yours once deletion completes.

Encrypted nightly database backups are kept for **35 days** and then deleted
automatically, so a copy of your data can survive in those backups for up to 35
days after deletion. They exist only to recover from a failure and are never read
to look anything up.

## Deleting some of your data without deleting your account

You do not need this page for that. Any expense, receipt image or shopping-list
entry can be deleted inside the app at any time, and it is removed for the whole
household.

You can also export everything you can see - profile, expenses, items, list,
categories and links to your receipt images - from **Settings → Export**, before
deleting anything.
